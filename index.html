ABOUT ME

Career support that starts with understanding people.

My work begins with understanding each client’s skills, experience, education, employment goals and the barriers affecting their next step.

Throughout my career, I have worked with newcomers, permanent residents, internationally trained professionals, youth, seniors and individuals facing multiple barriers to employment.

My experience includes comprehensive client intake and needs assessment, one-on-one career counselling, employment action planning, resume development, interview preparation, labour market research, employer outreach, referrals and post-placement support.

I believe effective employment services go beyond simply helping someone find a job. The goal is to help people understand their abilities, recognize realistic opportunities and make informed decisions about their future.
